Our programs & impact

From school fees and rescue support to business skills training, our programs meet children, youth, and women where they are — and help them build a self-reliant future.

20+

Children rescued and enrolled in school, with fees paid and essential school items provided.

50+

Youths reached with ongoing mentorship support.

20+

Women trained in business skills; 5+ received business start-up kits.

5+

Business start-up kits awarded to help youth and women launch small businesses.

Together, these numbers mean real households -- helped to create small businesses of their own through our business kits.

Rescue, education, essential & business skills support

Four ways we walk alongside children, youth, and women in Roysambu.

01

Rescue & education support

  • Rescue and educate vulnerable children.
  • Support children and youths returning to school (primary, secondary, and tertiary).
  • Support children of vulnerable families with uniforms and school fees.
  • Run a school and a rescue center.
02

Essential support

  • Distribute essential items for children in schools and the community.
  • Distribute food stuffs and clothes to vulnerable families in partnership with organizations and institutions.
  • Provide hot meals to vulnerable children in the community.
03

Business skills & economic opportunities

  • Provide business skills training in partnership with organizations.
  • Offer life skills training to empower 1,000 youth and women annually through mentorship programs.
  • Youth employment and entrepreneurship training.
